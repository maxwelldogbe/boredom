## How the word list is chosen

SCOWL sorts English words into size levels by commonness (10 = the most common, 95 = rare).
Those levels come from public-domain frequency lists (Moby Words II, Brian Kelk's UK English
frequency classes) and the public-domain 12Dicts lists. `build_words.py` uses the
`english-words.*` and `american-words.*` lists and:

- keeps lowercase a-z words of 4 to 9 letters with at least two different letters;
- sets difficulty by the documented length bands (easy 4-5, medium 6-7, hard 8-9 letters;
  PROTOCOL.md 7.2) and uses commonness to decide which words get in: easy and medium words
  up to level 35, hard words up to level 40, so rare and obscure words are left out;
- keeps an inflected form (plural, -ed, -ing, -er, -est, -ly) only when it is itself at
  level 20 or more common;
- drops abbreviations and contractions, and any word that is also a proper name unless it is
  at level 20 or more common (so "bill" stays, rarer name-words go);
- drops every word matched by `blocklist.txt`;
- drops a word if every arrangement of its letters is a listed or blocked word.

## How the emoji puzzles are chosen

- Only emoji from Emoji 13.1 or older, fully qualified, with no skin-tone modifiers and no ZWJ
  or tag sequences (`unicode_emoji.txt`). The server checks every puzzle against this table at
  startup.
- Single emoji are used only when the Unicode name gives one clear everyday answer; look-alikes
  (the many moons, clocks, arrows, buttons and faces) are left out.
- Flags are UN member states plus a few distinctive territories and the EU and UN flags.
  Flags that look the same on a phone (Chad/Romania, Indonesia/Monaco,
  Netherlands/Luxembourg) are left out. Capital puzzles skip countries with disputed, split or
  several capitals.
- Every answer is unique after normalisation, no two puzzles use the same emoji, and no answer
  contains a blocklisted word.

## Rebuilding

```sh
cd core
# Words: download and extract scowl-2020.12.07.tar.gz from
# https://sourceforge.net/projects/wordlist/files/SCOWL/2020.12.07/
python tools/build_words.py /path/to/scowl-2020.12.07
# Emoji: the table is regenerated only when emoji-test.txt is given
python tools/build_emoji.py /path/to/emoji-test.txt
python tools/validate_content.py
```
